# Privacy Policy

Last updated: `2026-07-10`

`Currency Converter` is a multi-currency converter with live exchange rates.

**Public URL (use this in Google Play / App Store):**  
https://stasholmansky.github.io/currency-converter-privacy/

## Who we are

For questions about this policy, contact:

- Email: `stanislavkholmanskii@gmail.com`

## What the app stores

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

- The list of currencies you selected for conversion
- Amounts you enter for conversion
- Cached exchange rates used for conversion
- Appearance preference (system / light / dark)

## Network requests

The app may contact the following third-party services:

1. `open.er-api.com` for fiat currency exchange rates
2. `api.coingecko.com` for selected cryptocurrency prices in USD

These services receive the minimum request data needed to return their responses. The app does not send your personal identity information to these services.

## Accounts and authentication

The app does not require an account and does not include sign-in.

## Analytics, ads, and tracking

The app does not include advertising SDKs or analytics/tracking SDKs in this repository version.

## Location

The app does not request location permission.

## Data sharing

Conversion data is not shared with other users. All conversion history stays on your device unless you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

## Data retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or clear app storage.

## Your choices

You can:

- Remove app data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage
- Change appearance settings at any time
